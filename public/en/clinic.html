Clinic

Özel DentTEK Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı Polikliniği

A private oral and dental health polyclinic on Nazım Hikmet Boulevard, at the Beylikdüzü–Esenyurt junction on the European side of Istanbul.

The clinic has examination and treatment units, a panoramic X-ray and a sterilisation unit. The waiting area is arranged so that families and children feel comfortable, and appointments are scheduled to keep waiting times short. Consultations can be held in English.

Hygiene and sterilisation

What happens between two patients

Individual instrument sets

The instruments used for each patient are prepared in sterile packs and opened in front of the patient. Single-use materials are discarded after the procedure.

Sterilisation cycle

After every use, instruments go through pre-cleaning, ultrasonic washing and pressurised-steam sterilisation in an autoclave; every cycle is logged.

Unit and surface hygiene

After each patient the unit, armrests, light handle and work surfaces are disinfected; water lines are cleaned regularly.

Imaging

A panoramic X-ray shows both jaws in one image; digital imaging uses a low dose and is taken only when needed.

Records and follow-up

Findings, plans and check-ups are kept in the patient file; patients are reminded when a check-up is due.

Accessibility

The clinic is on the ground floor with a wide entrance. Wheelchair access is possible; please mention it when booking.

Getting here

How to reach the clinic

The clinic is on the ground floor of the Altıntaş Okutan Business Centre, Nazım Hikmet Blv. No:89/2, Cumhuriyet district, Esenyurt. It is a few minutes from the TEM and E-5 (D-100) motorways and about 40–60 minutes by car from Istanbul Airport, 25–35 minutes from Beylikdüzü, Avcılar and Başakşehir. By public transport: Metrobüs to Beylikdüzü or Avcılar, then a bus or minibus towards Esenyurt along Cumhuriyet Street. Free street parking and car parks are available around the business centre.
