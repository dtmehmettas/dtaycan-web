Treatments

Areas of practice

The information below is for general guidance. Which treatment is right for you can only be decided after an examination.

✦

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers, zirconia crowns and composite bonding to improve the colour, shape and alignment of teeth while keeping a natural look.

More →
◎

Dental Implants

Titanium roots placed in the jawbone to replace missing teeth, with a fixed crown on top; neighbouring teeth are left untouched.

More →
≋

Orthodontics

Straightening crowded, spaced or misaligned teeth with clear aligners or braces; suitable for adults as well as children.

More →
☺

Pediatric Dentistry

Protecting milk teeth, fissure sealants and fluoride, treating decay and making the first visit stress-free.

More →
✧

Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening or home whitening with custom trays under dental supervision; lightening the shade without damaging enamel.

More →
⌘

Root Canal Treatment

Cleaning the inflamed or damaged nerve of the tooth and sealing the canals so that the tooth can be kept instead of extracted.

More →
❋

Gum Treatment

Scaling, root planing and gum contouring; most bleeding, recession and bad breath comes from the gums.

More →
◈

Prosthodontics

Fixed bridges, implant-supported prostheses and removable dentures to restore the function and appearance of lost teeth.

More →
◐

Fillings and Restorative Treatment

Tooth-coloured composite fillings, inlays/onlays and replacement of old fillings for decay and fractures.

More →
✚

Extractions and Oral Surgery

Extraction of teeth that cannot be saved, impacted wisdom teeth, root-tip surgery and minor soft-tissue procedures.
